Start with the way you’ll use the property

Before touring spaces, write down what the property needs to do for your business or investment. A shop, office, warehouse, service business and land purchase can call for very different features. Consider how much room you need now, whether you expect that to change, and what access customers, staff or deliveries will require.

It also helps to decide whether you’re looking to buy or rent. Ownership and leasing come with different commitments, and the right fit depends on your plans. A commercial real estate agency Longview clients contact can help identify available properties that fit the basics you share and coordinate visits so you can assess them in person.

Questions to ask before hiring

Ask what kinds of commercial properties the agency can help you search, how showings are arranged, and what support is available during negotiations and closing. You can also ask how the agent will keep you informed as options come up. Be clear about your desired location, timing and must-have features from the start.

A practical checklist for property visits

Bring a short list of requirements and use it consistently at each showing. Note what you can confirm on site, what needs follow-up and what might affect your intended use.

  • Check entrances, parking, loading areas and the route to the property.
  • Look at the layout, condition and improvements that matter to your operation.
  • Ask about utilities, access and any information you need to verify independently.
  • Take notes and photos where permitted, then compare each property against your priorities.
  • Identify questions about permitted use, costs or property condition for the right specialist.

Don’t rely on appearance alone. A space can look suitable but still need review for access, layout, condition or intended use. Confirm important details before making a commitment, and keep written records of questions and answers.

Avoid rushed decisions

A common mistake is touring properties without knowing which needs are essential and which are flexible. That can make it hard to compare options and may lead to spending time on a poor fit. Another is assuming a listing answers every question about a property. Ask for clarification, review the available information and seek professional guidance where needed.
