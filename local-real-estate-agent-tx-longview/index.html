What to discuss with a Longview agent

Before making a plan, tell an agent what you want the property to do for you. A home search may focus on daily needs and location, while land or a ranch can bring up questions about access, water, improvements and intended use. Commercial property decisions may call for a different set of considerations, such as how the space fits your business or investment plans.

Bring up your timing and any locations you have in mind. If you're selling, talk through your goals for the sale and what information you have about the property. If you're buying, share the features you consider essential and the ones you could compromise on. These details help keep conversations focused and property comparisons relevant.

A short checklist before you meet

A little preparation makes it easier to use a meeting or property showing well. Gather what you can, and note anything you still need to confirm.

  • Write down your preferred areas and property type.
  • List must-have features, along with items that are optional.
  • Note your intended use, especially for acreage or commercial property.
  • Collect details about improvements, access or water that you already know.
  • Prepare questions about showings, pricing, marketing or the contract process.

You don't need to have every answer before speaking with an agent. Being clear about what you know and what remains uncertain can help guide the next conversation.

Common missteps to avoid

Try not to judge a property only by its photos or asking price. Details that affect use, access or upkeep may not be clear until you ask questions or visit in person. For acreage, consider whether the land suits your plans rather than assuming every tract will work the same way. For a home or commercial space, think about how it will serve your needs beyond the first impression.

Another common misstep is leaving questions until late in a transaction. Ask about the process early, keep track of documents and respond promptly when decisions or signatures are needed.
